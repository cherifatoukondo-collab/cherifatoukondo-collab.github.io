Projects

Kénéya

Maternal and child health app, École Centrale Casablanca

A mobile app that reads a child’s health booklet with Llama 3.2 Vision, analyses fetal heart rate from smartphone audio and sends vaccination reminders. The model reaches over 90% accuracy.

A midwife told us some patients can’t read, so accessibility became part of the design.

INNO-Alert

Quality control for InnoFaso, 2iE, April to June 2026

On a Plumpy’Nut packaging line, our team of four built an Arduino Y-shaped double conveyor that counts 150 sachets per carton, plus a dashboard (Arduino, Flask, SQLite, React) with LLM-written alerts for micro-leaks.

Sankofa

Multi-role marketplace, Disgiscia, July to September 2026

As a full-stack developer intern, I built features for buyers, sellers and carriers, and wrote the technical documentation and a project audit.

Power outages in Burkina Faso

Africa Data Entry, July to August 2025

I analysed time series of electricity cuts and their socio-economic impact, cleaned data from several sources and built Power BI dashboards.

Predictive maintenance

Sensor data project

A project on predicting the remaining useful life (RUL) of equipment from sensor data.
